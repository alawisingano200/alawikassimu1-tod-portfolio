📖 English Notes - Standard Two

1. Demonstratives

Demonstratives are words used to point at people or things.

  • This: Used for one thing near us.
  • That: Used for one thing far from us.
  • These: Used for many things near us.
  • Those: Used for many things far from us.

Examples:

  • This is a book.
  • That is a tree.
  • These are pens.
  • Those are birds.

2. Prepositions of Place

Prepositions of place tell us where something or someone is.

  • In: Inside something.
  • On: On the surface of something.
  • Under: Below something.

Examples:

  • The book is in the bag.
  • The cup is on the table.
  • The ball is under the chair.

3. Has and Have

Has and Have show that someone owns something.

  • Use Has with He, She and It.
  • Use Have with I, You, We and They.

Examples:

  • She has a doll.
  • He has a bicycle.
  • I have a bag.
  • They have books.

4. Singular and Plural

Singular means one person or thing.

Plural means more than one person or thing.

We can add -s, -es or -ies to make some words plural.

  • Book → Books
  • Bus → Buses
  • Box → Boxes
  • Baby → Babies
  • City → Cities

5. Punctuation Marks

Punctuation marks are signs used in writing.

  • Full stop (.): Ends a statement.
  • Question mark (?): Ends a question.
  • Exclamation mark (!): Shows strong feelings.
  • Comma (,): Separates items in a list.

Examples:

  • I am a pupil.
  • What is your name?
  • Wow! That is beautiful!
  • I have a pen, a book and a bag.

🗣️ English Vocabulary

Words and Their Meanings

Learn these English words and their meanings.

  • Happy – Feeling good.
  • Sad – Feeling unhappy.
  • Beautiful – Very nice to look at.
  • Big – Large in size.
  • Small – Little in size.
  • Clean – Not dirty.
  • Dirty – Not clean.
  • Brave – Not afraid.
  • Quick – Fast.
  • Slow – Not fast.

Names of Things

  • Book – A thing we read.
  • Chair – A thing we sit on.
  • Table – A thing we put things on.
  • Pen – A thing we write with.
  • Bag – A thing we carry things in.
  • School – A place where pupils learn.

Action Words

  • Run – Move quickly.
  • Walk – Move on foot.
  • Eat – Take food.
  • Drink – Take water or another liquid.
  • Read – Look at and understand words.
  • Write – Make words or letters.
  • Sing – Make music with the voice.
  • Jump – Move up into the air.

Use the Words in Sentences

  • I am happy.
  • The elephant is big.
  • We read books at school.
  • Children like to play.
  • My mother is kind.

✍️ English Grammar

1. Nouns

A noun is a naming word. It names a person, animal, place or thing.

Examples:

  • Person – Teacher, mother, boy.
  • Animal – Dog, cat, cow.
  • Place – School, home, market.
  • Thing – Book, chair, pencil.

2. Pronouns

A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Examples:

  • I am a pupil.
  • You are my friend.
  • We are learning English.
  • They are playing.

3. Verbs

A verb is an action word.

Examples:

  • The boy runs.
  • Mother cooks food.
  • Children play football.
  • We read books.

4. Articles: A and An

We use A before a consonant sound.

We use An before a vowel sound.

Examples:

  • A boy.
  • A cat.
  • An egg.
  • An orange.

5. Making Simple Sentences

A sentence is a group of words that gives a complete meaning.

Examples:

  • I love my school.
  • She is reading a book.
  • We play together.
  • The teacher is kind.

📚 Reading Practice

Passage 1: My School

My name is Asha. I am a pupil. I go to Green Apple School. My school is beautiful. I have many friends. Our teachers teach us English, Mathematics and other subjects. I love my school.

Answer the Questions

  1. What is the name of the girl?
  2. Where does Asha study?
  3. Does Asha have friends?
  4. Which subjects does Asha learn?
  5. Does Asha love her school?

Passage 2: My Family

My name is Juma. I live with my family. My father is a teacher. My mother is a farmer. I have one brother and two sisters. We help each other at home. I love my family.

Answer the Questions

  1. What is the boy's name?
  2. Who is a teacher?
  3. What does his mother do?
  4. How many sisters does Juma have?
  5. Does Juma love his family?

Passage 3: The Importance of Cleanliness

Cleanliness is important in our lives. We should wash our hands before eating. We should brush our teeth every day. We should keep our homes and schools clean. Cleanliness helps us stay healthy.

Answer the Questions

  1. Is cleanliness important?
  2. When should we wash our hands?
  3. How often should we brush our teeth?
  4. Where should we keep clean?
  5. What does cleanliness help us to do?
