📘 Sample English Lessons

1. Demonstratives

We use this, that, these and those to point at people or things.

This is a book.

That is a tree.

These are pencils.

Those are birds.

2. Prepositions of Place

Prepositions tell us where something or someone is.

The book is on the table.

The cat is under the chair.

The pen is in the bag.

3. Has and Have

We use has with he, she and it. We use have with I, you, we and they.

She has a bag.

He has a ball.

I have a book.

They have pencils.

4. Singular and Plural

A singular noun names one person, animal or thing. A plural noun names more than one.

Book – Books

Box – Boxes

Baby – Babies

Mango – Mangoes

5. Punctuation Marks

Punctuation marks help us write sentences correctly.

Full stop (.) – I am a pupil.

Question mark (?) – What is your name?

Exclamation mark (!) – What a beautiful flower!

Comma (,) – I have a pen, a book and a bag.
